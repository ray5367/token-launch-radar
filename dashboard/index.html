How the value is figured

One function scores every row. No protocol name is special-cased, no row is hand-picked, and every input is a figure the snapshot itself carries.

  1. Price the category from the market. For each category, take every protocol in the snapshot whose token already trades and divide its market cap by its TVL. The category multiple is the median of those ratios — medians, so one outlier cannot move the price. Categories with fewer than 5 token protocols use the global median instead, and the row says which.
  2. Reward momentum, capped. Seven-day TVL change is clamped to −50%…+100% and half its weight is applied: growth factor = 1 + 0.5 × clamped change. A protocol shrinking 50% keeps 0.75×; one doubling gets 1.5×. No report means no change.
  3. Reward a long no-token track record, on a log scale. Age is the days from first listing to the snapshot date, capped at 1,095 days. Age factor = 0.65 + 0.35 × ln(1 + age) / ln(1 + cap): the first year counts more than the fourth. A missing listing date uses the eligible set's median age, figured from the snapshot.
  4. Multiply and rank. implied = TVL × multiple × growth factor × age factor, rounded to whole dollars. Ties break by slug, so the ranking is stable and reproducible.

What is left out, and why

Excluded from the ranking: any protocol that records a token (a market cap, a CoinGecko id, or a symbol), and any wrapped, staked, bridged or versioned product — Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC and CEX categories, names carrying a whole word wrapped, staked or staking, names ending in a version marker such as V2, and names beginning with an exchange name. A wrapped product of a token that already trades is not a launch waiting to happen.

Farm plans

Each row's plan is generated from that protocol's own category, chains, TVL and age: the category picks the venue and the risk, the TVL picks the capital, the first chain picks the gas budget, the age picks the holding period. Steps name amounts, costs and time. They are the standard farming playbook for the category and chain — the snapshot carries no points program, and no plan claims one exists.
